# World Integration Guidelines

## Purpose

This document explains how the world of Dissonance should show up in gameplay, space, audio, and progression without turning implementation documents into lore dumps.

## Core Rule

Core mechanics should have a believable in-world explanation tied to:

- Synod infrastructure
- SignalNet interpretation
- suppression systems
- surveillance retrofits
- decay, drift, and partial system failure

If a mechanic cannot be explained through the world, it should be reconsidered or reframed.

## World Pillars

### The Synod

The Synod is not just a government backdrop. It should be felt as an organizing force that shapes architecture, behavior, and permissible expression.

### SignalNet

SignalNet is the distributed surveillance substrate. It should imply pattern analysis, anomaly detection, and behavioral interpretation rather than simple camera watching.

### Suppressed Expression

Music, ornamental sound, and non-functional harmonic structures are restricted, taboo, or dangerous. Sound is therefore both a risk and a latent form of resistance.

### Authoritarian Residue

The world should show retrofits, degradation, patched systems, and outdated control layers. Synod power should feel pervasive, but not pristine.

## Gameplay Translation

### Stealth

Stealth should imply:

- avoiding visual confirmation
- avoiding suspicious sound
- avoiding behavioral irregularity
- managing repeated risky actions that make the player easier to classify

Escalation should imply:

- observation
- interpretation
- tagging
- pursuit
- containment

### State Model

The player state should feel like overload under interpretation pressure.

Pressure sources can include:

- time in exposed zones
- proximity to drones
- signal emitters or dense surveillance infrastructure
- excessive sound or irregular sound
- sustained pursuit pressure

### Audio

Audio should map to fiction:

- footsteps are bodily evidence
- drone tones are behavioral control signals
- warnings and shrieks are coercive sound, not generic alarm content
- safe spaces should sound meaningfully different from controlled spaces
- harmony should feel rare enough that it carries emotional and political meaning

## Environmental Storytelling Guidance

Use environmental details that imply control and decay with minimal exposition:

- signage with partial corruption or outdated directives
- public-address loops that still repeat obsolete rules
- architectural choke points converted into observation funnels
- sound-dampened alcoves or rooms suggesting enforced acoustic control
- maintenance patchwork around surveillance infrastructure
- hidden marks, symbols, or fragments implying erased musical culture

## Progression Philosophy

Progression should emerge from the world’s machinery.

Good examples:

- salvaging drone or SignalNet hardware for player tools
- reclaiming parts that were meant for compliance and turning them into concealment or jamming tools
- finding forbidden symbols or audio fragments that gradually reopen expressive possibility

The intended thematic line is:

- sound begins as exposure
- sound becomes technique
- sound becomes identity and resistance

## Early Slice Hook

A strong early milestone is a progression beat where the player:

- brings down or disables a drone
- salvages parts from it
- repurposes those parts into an anti-detection audio jammer

This is a good model because it ties progression, fiction, stealth, and resistance together through one concrete action.

## Future-Facing Hooks

These ideas should influence current planning even if they are not fully implemented yet:

- rogue or modified drones
- non-Synod signal systems
- outlawed instruments or reconstructed instruments
- songs, motifs, or structures built from found fragments
- resistance tools built from surveillance debris

## Implementation Guidance

When designing a new system, ask:

1. What part of the world makes this system believable?
2. How does Synod control or SignalNet interpretation shape it?
3. What environmental evidence supports it?
4. Does it push the player from concealment toward expression in a meaningful way?

If those answers are weak, the system likely needs more world integration.
